## Problem
Greenleaf store managers check four different reports every morning. It takes \~40 minutes and stock problems are often noticed too late.
## Goals
- One dashboard with daily sales, top 10 products and low-stock alerts
- Loads in under 2 seconds
- Works on a tablet in the store
## Non-goals (beta)
- Forecasting
- Multi-language support (planned for v1.1: Hebrew and English)
## Success metrics
- Managers spend under 10 minutes on morning reports
- 80% of pilot managers use it at least 4 days a week
